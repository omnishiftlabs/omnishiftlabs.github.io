About this policy

OmniShift Studios develops CoastRush: Traffic Racing. This policy explains information handled by the game and the third-party services it uses. The game is intended for a teen and adult audience.

Game information stored on your device

The game stores Career progress, unlocked cars, tuning, coins, preferences, local scores, Daily challenge results, recent run history and reward-claim records on your device. These records support gameplay, progression, rewards and recovery after interruptions. The current game does not upload this run history to an OmniShift analytics or Daily challenge server. Android device backup settings may affect whether app data is backed up by your device provider.

Advertising and privacy choices

The game integrates Google AdMob for optional rewarded advertising. Depending on availability and your choices, you may choose an ad for a Quick Run rescue or a coin bonus. Normal play and retry remain available without accepting a rewarded offer.

When the advertising service operates, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process IP address (which can indicate approximate location), advertising and other device or account identifiers, ad and app interactions, and diagnostic information. This information supports ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and, where permitted and selected, ad personalization. The game does not request precise device location for gameplay.

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform presents privacy choices before eligible ad requests. You can revisit available choices through Ad Privacy Options in the game settings. You can also manage your advertising ID and advertising preferences through Android and Google account settings. Choosing non-personalized advertising does not necessarily stop processing needed to deliver and measure ads or prevent fraud. Ad availability may depend on your privacy choices.

Google explains its practices at https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Google Play Games

The Android game includes Google Play Games integration. Where enabled and used, Google may process your Play Games player ID, display name, profile and submitted scores for sign-in and leaderboard features. Your gamer identity and scores may be visible to other players according to the service's settings. Builds without a configured online leaderboard use local records instead. Google account and Play Games information is governed by Google's policies and your Google settings.

Privacy-policy website

This policy is published using GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. The page does not include advertising tags or separate website analytics. See GitHub's privacy statement for its handling of website visitor information.

Contacting us

If you email omnishiftstudios@gmail.com, we receive your email address and anything you choose to include. We use this information to respond and resolve your request. Please do not send passwords, payment details or other information unnecessary for support.

Sharing, storage and retention

Information is processed by Google and its service partners as described above and when you contact us through email. Service providers may process information in countries outside your own under their applicable policies. We may disclose information when legally required or necessary to address abuse or protect rights.

Local game records remain until cleared or removed, subject to device backup behavior. We retain support correspondence for as long as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations, then delete or anonymize it when no longer needed. Google and other providers manage their own retention under their policies. We do not set or promise a retention period for records held independently by Google.

Your choices and requests

You can clear the game's local data through Android app settings; this also removes local progress and rewards. Clearing local data does not automatically delete information held by Google or device backups. Manage those records through the relevant Google account, Play Games, backup and advertising controls.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, object to or restrict certain processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a data-protection authority. Email omnishiftstudios@gmail.com for requests concerning information OmniShift Studios holds. We may need enough information to verify and respond to your request. Requests for independently held Google account or advertising information should also use Google's privacy controls.

Younger players

The game is designed for teens and adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly seek personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact omnishiftstudios@gmail.com so we can investigate and address it. Regional age and consent requirements may differ; an audience label alone does not determine advertising eligibility.

Changes

We will update this policy when the game's information practices change. The published policy will state its effective or last-updated date. Material changes will be communicated where required.
